The renewal pipeline

Every renewal follows the same spine.

Not a storyboard. These are the real case states the app persists, transitions and counts — src/lib/workflow/renewal.ts defines the moves, /admin/workflow draws them live, and the funnel reads them back.

scored

Risk tier and renewal window open automatically.

reminded

The campaign skips anyone without consent, and logs why.

engaged

The driver starts — on whichever channel they chose.

confirmed

Vehicle plate and make confirmed by the customer, never guessed.

paid

Regulated premium, priced from the tariff. Never discounted.

issued

Certificate written, QR verifiable at its own public URL.

Read this before you judge

What actually works, and what is theatre.

A prototype that oversells itself is worth less than one that is honest about its seams. Here is exactly where the line sits.

Genuinely implemented

real logic, real state

  • Rule-based lead scoring and the regulated premium tariff
  • Consent-gated campaigns with exclusion accounting
  • Append-only audit ledger; read-only to compliance
  • Per-route RBAC and PII masking until reveal-with-reason
  • Renewal workflow transitions shared by app and visualiser
  • Certificate issuance and public QR verification
  • Vietnamese / English toggle across every surface
  • Multiplayer presence across all three rooms

Deliberately simulated

labelled in the UI

  • Payment capture — no gateway is connected
  • Issuance — no insurer API, certificates are synthetic
  • Claims — photo upload and stub triage only
  • The AI agent — deterministic replies unless you add API keys
  • ZNS templates and VETC records — hand-written fixtures
  • All customers, policies and premiums — invented data

Under the hood

Built to be extended, not admired.

The whole app is a single Next.js project under demo/. Add a provider key and the agent switches from scripted replies to a live model with no other change.
